**Pitch:**  
"I built Mod Cellular — it's like Helium but for communication. Your phone relays messages even without cell service, and you earn crypto for it."

"Here's my card. Let's grab coffee and I'll show you a demo."

## 2-MINUTE VERSION (Coffee Meeting, Scheduled Call, Formal Pitch)

**Context:** You have someone's dedicated attention for a brief meeting. Time to go deeper.

**Pitch:**  
"Thanks for taking the time. I'm Jason Harris, and I'm building Mod Cellular — the first decentralized communication network that works without cell service.

**The Problem:**  
Right now, 6.9 billion people rely on three companies — AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile — for connectivity. When you're in a dead zone, at a protest, or during a natural disaster, you lose the ability to communicate. Cell towers are centralized points of failure, and carriers charge users instead of rewarding them.

**Our Solution:**  
Mod Cellular routes messages through WiFi, Bluetooth mesh, and satellite fallback. If one fails, we automatically switch to another. Messages are split into 250-byte micro-packets and sent across multiple paths. We also have DTN — Delay-Tolerant Networking — so messages queue offline for up to 7 days and deliver when you reconnect.

**The Economics:**  
Here's where it gets interesting: we use a DePIN model — Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks. Users earn MODX tokens for relaying messages. This is the same model Helium used to reach a billion-dollar valuation for IoT connectivity. We're applying it to communication, which is a $1.6 trillion market — 30 times larger than IoT.

**Traction:**  
We're pre-launch with 25,000 waitlist signups, growing 40% week-over-week. Our early community is highly engaged — 18% referral rate, which signals strong product-market fit.

**Go-to-Market:**  
Beta launches Q1 2026 with 1,000 users in high-density cities. Public launch Q2 on iOS and Android. Token launch Q3 with DEX listing. By end of year, we're targeting 2 million users and $35 million ARR.

**The Ask:**  
We're raising a $2 million seed round at a $12 million pre-money valuation. Capital goes toward mobile engineering, token launch infrastructure, and initial marketing spend. We'd love to have you as part of this.

Can I send over our deck and financials?"

**Pause. Listen. Answer questions.**

## RESPONSE TO COMMON QUESTIONS

### "How is this different from WhatsApp?"
"WhatsApp requires internet or cell service. Mod Cellular works via WiFi, Bluetooth mesh, and satellite — even in complete dead zones. Plus, you earn tokens for relaying, whereas WhatsApp users pay with their data."

### "How do you make money?"
"Token appreciation — as the network grows, MODX value increases. Premium features — $4.99/month for HD calls and priority routing. Enterprise licensing — military, government, and corporate deployments. And eventually hardware partnerships."

### "What if no one is nearby to relay?"
"Messages stay in your DTN queue for up to 7 days. When you reconnect to ANY network — WiFi at a cafe, Bluetooth from a passing car, satellite signal — they're automatically sent. 94.7% deliver within 24 hours."

### "Isn't this just Helium?"
"Helium focuses on IoT device connectivity. We focus on human communication — messaging, calls, and media. The TAM is 30 times larger. We're also using different infrastructure: Bluetooth mesh instead of LoRaWAN, and satellite fallback for global coverage."

### "How do you prevent spam?"
"Reputation scoring. Users with low scores (frequent spam, low-quality relays) earn fewer tokens and get deprioritized in routing. High-reputation users earn more and get priority. It's self-regulating."

## DELIVERY TIPS

### Pacing
- Speak clearly, not rushed
- Pause after key points ("...Helium model. [pause] $1.6 trillion market. [pause]")
- Match energy to audience (investors = energetic, partners = professional, press = conversational)

### Body Language
- Eye contact (70% of the time, not constant)
- Open posture (no crossed arms)
- Minimal gestures (use hands to emphasize points, not constant motion)

### Tone
- Confident, not arrogant
- Passionate, not desperate
- Knowledgeable, not condescending

### Practice
- Rehearse 10+ times before real pitches
- Record yourself, watch playback
- Get feedback from peers
- Adjust based on reactions
