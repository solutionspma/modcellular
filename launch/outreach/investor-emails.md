## DO'S AND DON'TS

### DO:
✅ Personalize every email (mention recent investment, article, tweet)  
✅ Keep emails under 150 words (busy people skim)  
✅ Lead with traction (signups, growth rate, referrals)  
✅ Make it easy to say yes (specific time/date for call)  
✅ Follow up 2-3 times (persistence shows conviction)  

### DON'T:
❌ Send generic mass emails (instant delete)  
❌ Overpromise or exaggerate traction  
❌ Attach deck in first email (too much friction)  
❌ Use buzzwords without substance ("revolutionary," "disruptive")  
❌ Give up after one email (average response rate: 5-10%)
