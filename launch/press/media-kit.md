## Product Specifications

### Technical Details
- **Platform:** iOS 13+ | Android 8+
- **Framework:** React Native (Expo 51)
- **Blockchain:** Ethereum, Polygon, Base
- **Token Standard:** ERC-20
- **File Size:** ~85 MB (iOS), ~92 MB (Android)
- **Languages:** English (more coming)

### Supported Connections
- WiFi (802.11 b/g/n/ac/ax)
- Bluetooth 4.0+ (mesh mode)
- Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)
- GPS/GLONASS (satellite opportunistic mode)
- LTE/5G (fallback when available)

### Message Types
- Text (unlimited characters, micro-packet split)
- Images (JPEG, PNG, up to 10 MB)
- Videos (MP4, up to 100 MB)
- Voice messages (up to 60 seconds)
- Voice calls (1-on-1 or group, up to 8 participants)

## Quote Library

### On the Problem
> "Every time I see 'No Service' on my phone, I'm reminded that 6.9 billion people depend on 3 companies. That's not a network — that's a monopoly."

> "Cell towers are centralized points of failure. One hurricane, one protest, one outage — and millions lose connectivity. We need infrastructure that's resilient by design."

### On the Solution
> "Mod Cellular is telecom without telecom companies. You message via WiFi, Bluetooth, mesh, satellite — whatever works. And you earn tokens for relaying, so the network pays you instead of the other way around."

> "We're applying the Helium DePIN model to communication. Helium proved you can build infrastructure through token incentives. We're doing the same thing for the $1.6 trillion telecom market."

### On Token Economics
> "Your phone becomes infrastructure. You're not just a customer; you're an owner earning revenue from the network."

> "The more people relay messages, the stronger the network gets, the more valuable MODX becomes. It's a flywheel that rewards early adopters."

### On Privacy
> "Traditional carriers see everything: who you message, when, where. We see nothing. End-to-end encryption means only sender and recipient can decrypt messages."

> "No backdoors, no metadata collection, no government surveillance. If you want privacy, decentralization is the only way."

### On Emergencies
> "In a natural disaster, when AT&T and Verizon go dark, Mod Cellular keeps working. That's the difference between centralized and decentralized."

> "Emergency SOS broadcasts to all nearby devices at priority 999, even without internet. In a crisis, this could save lives."

### On the Future
> "Our long-term vision is to replace carriers entirely. Imagine a world where you never pay a phone bill again because you're earning from the network."

> "This is bigger than an app. This is a movement to democratize telecom infrastructure. Users should own their connectivity."
