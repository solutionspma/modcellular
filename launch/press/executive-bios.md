**Long Bio (300 words)**

Jason Harris is the founder and CEO of Mod Cellular, a decentralized communication platform that's redefining how people stay connected. Launched in 2025, Mod Cellular routes messages through WiFi, Bluetooth mesh, and satellite fallback — eliminating dependency on traditional carriers like AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile.

**The Origin Story**

The idea for Mod Cellular came to Jason during [personal experience — to be filled with actual story]. Frustrated by dropped calls, dead zones, and the oligopolistic control of the telecom industry, he asked a simple question: *What if phones could talk to each other directly?*

That question led to 18 months of research into mesh networking, delay-tolerant networking (DTN), and blockchain-based incentive structures. Jason studied Helium's DePIN model — which rewarded users for building IoT infrastructure — and realized the same flywheel could be applied to communication, a far larger market.
