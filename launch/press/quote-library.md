## On Technology

> "We split messages into 250-byte micro-packets and route them across multiple links. If one path fails, others keep going."

> "Adaptive bandwidth means quality scales from 10% to 100% based on signal. You always stay connected, even if it's slower."

> "DTN — Delay-Tolerant Networking — is how NASA communicates with Mars rovers. We're bringing that to everyday messaging."

> "Bluetooth mesh can relay messages up to 8 hops away. That's a potential reach of several miles without any internet."
