# 3-Year Expansion Plan
## Mod Cellular Growth Roadmap (2026-2028)

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** November 18, 2025  
**Goal:** 10M users globally by 2028

---

## Executive Summary

This document outlines Mod Cellular's strategic expansion from 50K users (Q1 2026) to 10M users (Q4 2028) across three phases:

1. **Year 1 (2026): US Dominance** - 250K users, $30M ARR, break-even
2. **Year 2 (2027): North America + EU** - 2M users, $240M ARR, profitable
3. **Year 3 (2028): Global Scale** - 10M users, $1.2B ARR, category leader

## Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Regulatory Delays

**Risk:** FCC/EU approval takes longer than expected  
**Mitigation:** Hire ex-regulators, over-comply early, budget 2x time

### Risk 2: Carrier Retaliation

**Risk:** AT&T/Verizon lobby to block us  
**Mitigation:** Position as "pro-competition," leverage FCC Open Internet rules

### Risk 3: Technology Failure

**Risk:** Mesh network doesn't scale  
**Mitigation:** Hybrid model (cellular + mesh), not mesh-only

### Risk 4: Market Timing

**Risk:** Users not ready for decentralized telecom  
**Mitigation:** Privacy concerns are growing, timing is perfect now
