## Emergency Calling Notice

Mod Cellular provides E911 emergency calling service when your phone number is registered to a valid physical address. **E911 service through Mod Cellular differs from traditional wireless 911 service and has important limitations.**

---

## How E911 Works with Mod Cellular

### Address Registration Required

To use E911 service, you must:
1. Register a valid physical address in your account
2. Keep this address current at all times
3. Update your address within 24 hours of moving

**Your registered address will be transmitted to emergency services when you call 911.**

### Location Accuracy

Unlike traditional cell phone 911:
- **Mod Cellular does NOT automatically detect your location**
- Emergency responders receive your **registered address**, not your current location
- If you call 911 away from your registered address, responders will go to your registered address
- **You must verbally provide your actual location when calling 911**

---

## Critical Limitations

### 1. Address-Based Routing

**LIMITATION:** Your 911 call is routed based on your **registered address**, not your current location.

**CONSEQUENCE:** If you are traveling and call 911, your call may be routed to the wrong emergency services center.

**MITIGATION:** Always state your current location verbally when calling 911.

### 2. No Automatic Location Detection

**LIMITATION:** Mod Cellular cannot automatically send your GPS coordinates to 911.

**CONSEQUENCE:** If you cannot speak during a 911 call, emergency responders will only know your registered address.

**MITIGATION:** 
- Keep your registered address current
- Consider also calling 911 from a traditional cell phone if available
- Enable location services on your device

### 3. Power Outage Impact

**LIMITATION:** Mod Cellular requires internet connection (WiFi or cellular data).

**CONSEQUENCE:** During power outages, WiFi may not be available. If your phone has no cellular data, you cannot call 911 through Mod Cellular.

**MITIGATION:**
- Keep a backup battery for your WiFi router
- Ensure your phone has cellular data as backup
- Consider keeping a traditional phone line for emergencies

### 4. Network Dependency

**LIMITATION:** E911 service depends on:
- Internet connection (WiFi or cellular data)
- Mod Cellular servers being operational
- Telnyx carrier connectivity

**CONSEQUENCE:** Service interruptions may prevent 911 calls.

**MITIGATION:**
- Keep a traditional phone option available
- Monitor service status at status.modcellular.network

### 5. Delayed Routing

**LIMITATION:** VoIP 911 calls may take longer to route than traditional 911 calls.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Emergency response may be delayed by 10-30 seconds.

**MITIGATION:**
- Stay calm and remain on the line
- Provide clear verbal location information immediately

## Address Registration Process

### Initial Setup (Required Before First Call)

1. **Open Mod Cellular App**
2. **Go to Settings → Emergency Address**
3. **Enter Your Physical Address:**
   - Street address (including apartment/unit)
   - City
   - State
   - ZIP code

4. **Verify Address:**
   - App will validate with USPS database
   - Correct any errors flagged by validation
   - Confirm geocoded location on map

5. **Accept E911 Disclosure:**
   - Read this entire document
   - Check "I understand the limitations"
   - Click "Activate E911 Service"

### Moving to a New Address

1. **Update Immediately** (within 24 hours)
2. **Go to Settings → Emergency Address**
3. **Click "Update Address"**
4. **Enter New Address**
5. **Reverify and Confirm**

**WARNING:** Failure to update your address may result in emergency services being dispatched to your old address.

### Temporary Relocation

If you are temporarily away from your registered address (vacation, business trip):

**Option 1:** Update your address temporarily
- Change address to current location
- Change back when you return

**Option 2:** Use traditional phone for 911
- Use hotel phone, landline, or traditional cell service
- Mod Cellular 911 will route to your registered address

## What Happens When You Call 911

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **You Dial 911 in Mod Cellular**
2. **App retrieves your registered E911 address**
3. **Call routes to appropriate PSAP** (Public Safety Answering Point)
4. **PSAP receives:**
   - Your phone number
   - Your registered address
   - Your name (if provided)
5. **Dispatcher answers and requests information**
6. **You provide:**
   - Nature of emergency
   - **Your actual current location** (if different from registered address)
   - Any other requested information

### If You Have No Registered Address

**SCENARIO:** You call 911 without a registered address.

**WHAT HAPPENS:**
- Call routes to a national emergency center (NOT your local 911)
- National center attempts to determine your location
- Call may be transferred to local PSAP (adding delay)
- Response time may be significantly delayed

**REQUIREMENT:** You **must** register an address before calling 911 through Mod Cellular.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I call 911 while traveling?
**A:** Yes, but emergency services will be dispatched to your **registered address** unless you verbally provide your current location. Update your address if you're relocating temporarily.

### Q: What if my internet is down?
**A:** Without internet (WiFi or cellular data), you cannot call 911 through Mod Cellular. Use a traditional phone.

### Q: Will 911 see my location automatically?
**A:** No. They will see your **registered address only**. You must verbally state your actual location.

### Q: What if I can't speak during a 911 call?
**A:** Emergency services will dispatch to your registered address. This is why keeping your address current is critical.

### Q: Can I use Mod Cellular as my only phone?
**A:** While possible, we recommend keeping a backup phone for emergencies, especially if you travel frequently.

### Q: What if I forget to update my address?
**A:** Emergency services may be dispatched to the wrong location. You may be held liable for false dispatch costs.
