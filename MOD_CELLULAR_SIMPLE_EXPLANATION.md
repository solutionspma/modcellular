# Mod Cellular — The Simple Explanation

## What is it?

**A messaging and calling app that works:**
- Without a phone plan
- Without a SIM card  
- Without cell service

All it needs is any little bit of Wi-Fi or a nearby device running Mod Cellular.

---

## How does it work?

It constantly looks for:
- Free Wi-Fi
- Open hotspots
- Bluetooth connections
- Other Mod Cellular users
- Weak signals
- Rogue access points

If it finds anything, it uses that to:
- Send messages
- Send voice
- Send videos
- Make calls

It does this by **breaking data into tiny micro-packets**, so even a weak or crappy connection works.

---

## What if there is no connection at all?

It **stores your message** and sends it later the second ANY connection appears.

This is called: **DTN (Delay-Tolerant Networking)**.

---

## What if we're in the wilderness?

If another Mod Cellular phone is nearby:
- The phones create a **mesh network**
- They pass messages between each other
- The message keeps hopping until it finds a node with internet

This is **mesh relay messaging**.

---

## How do people get paid?

Mod Cellular pays **tokens** to users when their phone:
- Relays someone else's message
- Forwards packets
- Helps other devices get connected
- Stays online as part of the network

This is called: **Relay Rewards**.

Your phone becomes a **paid infrastructure node**.
